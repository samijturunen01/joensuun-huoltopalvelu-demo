# Image replacement list

**No temporary or placeholder photographs are used.** Every photo on the site is the
company's own rating-plate photograph from joensuunhuoltopalvelu.fi, and all other
visuals are custom SVG/CSS built from the authentic logo.

The design deliberately turns the company's real material – the logo's washing-machine
mark and the plate photos – into the visual language, so nothing has to be replaced
before production.
